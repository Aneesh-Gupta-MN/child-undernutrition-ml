# Draft DHS data-access request

## Project title

Questionnaire-based estimation of current childhood stunting in India: an NFHS-5 machine-learning feasibility study

## Proposed analysis

This academic project will investigate whether a small set of demographic and household questionnaire variables can estimate current stunting among children aged 6–59 months using India NFHS-5 (2019–21) Children's Recode data. Candidate predictors include child age, sex, birth order, maternal education, household membership count, drinking-water source, toilet facility and rural/urban residence. Height-for-age data will be used to define the outcome, not as a model predictor.

We plan to compare a simple baseline, regularized logistic regression and one tree-based model. The analysis will document sample eligibility, missingness and exclusions; keep households and survey clusters together during validation; and include a geographic holdout. Evaluation will examine discrimination, sensitivity, precision and probability calibration, with attention to survey weights and performance across age, sex and geographic groups.

This is a secondary-data feasibility study of current nutritional status, not a forecast of future malnutrition or a clinically validated decision tool. The work will report aggregate findings and limitations, without attempting to identify respondents or redistributing the microdata.
